# Care Path Patient Transport — Design System

## Brand
- Company: Care Path Patient Transport
- Domain: carepathpatienttransport.co.uk
- Email: info@carepathpatienttransport.co.uk

## Colours
- Primary Teal: #2BAD8A
- Accent Orange: #F5923A
- Deep Navy: #0B1F3A
- Light Teal BG: #EEF9F5
- Mid Grey: #6B7280
- Light Grey BG: #F8FAFB
- White: #FFFFFF

## Typography
- Display/Headings: 'Plus Jakarta Sans' (bold, strong)
- Body: 'DM Sans' (clean, readable)
- Both from Google Fonts

## Style
- Clean, modern, professional — healthcare authority + warmth
- Hero: Full-width, dark navy overlay on a medical transport image, bold headline
- Cards: Subtle shadow, rounded corners, teal accent borders on hover
- Buttons: Primary = teal fill, white text; Secondary = orange fill, white text
- Section backgrounds alternate: white → light teal → white → navy
- Icons: Simple line icons (lucide-react)
- Motion: Subtle fade-in on scroll for sections

## Sections (single page)
1. Nav — logo left, links right, "Book Now" CTA button (orange)
2. Hero — bold headline, subtext, two CTAs, background image with navy overlay
3. Services — 6 service cards with icons
4. Why Choose Us — 4 trust pillars with icons
5. About — short company description, values
6. Booking/Contact Form — Name, Email, Phone, Service Type, Message
7. Footer — logo, email, placeholder phone/address, nav links, copyright
